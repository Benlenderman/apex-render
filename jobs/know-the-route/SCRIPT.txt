s1|Leading a group? Never lead a route you've never ridden.
s2|You miss a turn, brake hard to make it, and the whole group follows you into the mistake.
s3|Ride it first, or study it on the map: the turns, the fuel stops, the rough parts.
s4|Share the route before the ride, so every rider has it on their phone.
s5|Not sure about a turn? Slow down early and signal early.
s6|The group rides only as well as you lead.
s7|Know the route. Share the route.
