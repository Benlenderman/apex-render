s1|Your friend crashed and says they're fine. Never let them ride straight on.
s2|Adrenaline hides pain. A broken wrist or a knock to the head can feel like nothing for a while.
s3|Sit them down. Take ten minutes. Ask where it hurts, and watch how they move.
s4|Check their helmet for a hit, and their bike for bent levers and brakes.
s5|If they hit their head, seem confused, or something hurts, they don't ride. Get help.
s6|The bike can be collected later. Your friend comes first.
s7|Sit down first. Ride later, or not at all.
