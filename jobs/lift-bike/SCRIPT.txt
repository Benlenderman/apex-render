s1|A friend dropped their bike? Never lift it by bending over.
s2|You pull with your back, the bike is heavier than you think, and your back goes.
s3|Put the bike in gear, and turn the bars toward the ground.
s4|Sit your back against the seat. One hand on the bar, one under the rear.
s5|Push with your legs, small steps backwards. If it lies on its right side, put the stand down first.
s6|Or lift it together. Two people, no injuries.
s7|Back to the seat. Lift with your legs.
